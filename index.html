HOW TO PLAY.

THE OBJECTIVE

Finish six rounds with the most coins. Start with 100. Investments settle each round, so your liquid balance is your wealth.

THE ROUND

Read market signals and events, negotiate for 15 seconds, then lock your actions during a 20-second planning window. Results reveal market movement, investment returns, sabotage, and transfers.

THE ASSETS

Ember 🔥 volatile growth · Tide 🌊 balanced movement · Vault 💎 defensive opportunity. Public signals hint at movement; Oracle reveals one exact move.

YOUR ACTIONS

Invest: up to 60 coins total across assets. Protect: 5 coins per token, up to two; offset a market loss by 10 points. Sabotage: 10 coins to disrupt an investment or attempt theft of up to 5 coins. Alliance: both players must choose each other; lasts one round. Transfer: send 1–20 coins to one player.

SPECIAL ABILITIES

Guardian: extra protection. Phantom: stronger sabotage. Oracle: exact movement clue. Diplomat: mutual pact can earn each player 5 coins. Tycoon: +10 coins. Sentinel: block one theft. Each ability is used once per match.

FAIR PLAY

Actions lock simultaneously. No borrowing or negative balances. Market outcomes are deterministic after signals are generated. If you disconnect, your seat and submitted actions are preserved; if no action is submitted, you take no optional actions.
